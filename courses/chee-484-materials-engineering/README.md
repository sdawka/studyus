# CHEE 484 — Materials Engineering

- Credits: 3
- Term: Winter 2025
- Instructor: Anne-Marie Kietzig
- Prereqs/coreqs/restrictions: Prerequisite: CHEE 380
- Official source: https://www.mcgill.ca/study/2024-2025/courses/chee-484

## Official overview
Processes for forming and producing engineering materials such as amorphous, semicrystalline, textured and crystal-oriented substances and composites. Phase transformations, nucleation and growth. Effect of processing variables on finished-article properties. Blending/alloying. Shaping and joining operations. Vessel equipment design for chemical engineering applications.
