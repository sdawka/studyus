# CHEE 351 — Separation Processes

- Credits: 3
- Term: Winter 2025
- Instructor: Ali Seifitokaldani
- Prereqs/coreqs/restrictions: Prerequisite: CHEE 220. Corequisites: CHEE 204 and CHEE 315
- Official source: https://www.mcgill.ca/study/2024-2025/courses/chee-351

## Official overview
Equilibrium-based separation; design of processes and equipment for distillation, absorption/stripping, liquid extraction, washing, and leaching. Consideration of mass transfer effects.
